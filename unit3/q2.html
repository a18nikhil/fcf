Q2. List the Steps Involved in Evidence Processing and Report Preparation in Forensic Investigation.

Introduction

Evidence processing means collecting, protecting, and checking digital evidence during an investigation.

Report preparation means writing a report about the evidence collected and the results of the investigation.

1. Steps Involved in Evidence Processing

1. Find Evidence

Find devices that may contain evidence, such as computers, mobile phones, hard disks, and USB drives.

2. Collect Evidence

Carefully collect the devices and digital evidence by following legal rules.

3. Protect Evidence

Keep the original evidence safe from changes or damage.

Make forensic copies and compare the hash values of the original and copied data to check that they match.

4. Examine Evidence

Use forensic tools to check files, emails, browser history, and deleted data.

5. Analyze Evidence

Study the evidence to understand what happened, how it happened, and who may be involved.

6. Maintain Chain of Custody

Keep a record of who collected, handled, transferred, and stored the evidence throughout the investigation.

This helps show that the evidence was handled properly.

2. Steps Involved in Report Preparation

1. Write Case Details

Write the case number, date, investigator's name, and reason for the investigation.

2. List the Evidence

Write down the devices and digital evidence collected during the investigation.

3. Explain the Methods Used

Mention the forensic tools and methods used to check the evidence.

4. Write the Findings

Clearly explain the important information found during the investigation.

Add screenshots or other supporting evidence when needed.

5. Write the Conclusion

Write a short summary of what the investigation found based on the evidence.

6. Check and Submit the Report

Check the report for mistakes and missing information.

Sign and submit the final report to the proper authority.
