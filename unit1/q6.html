Q6. Safe Boxes and Digital Vaults

Introduction

Safe boxes and digital vaults are used in cyber forensics to protect digital devices and stored evidence.

They keep evidence safe from theft, damage, and changes.

1. Physical Safe Boxes

Physical safe boxes are locked boxes used to store devices like hard disks, pen drives, memory cards, and mobile phones.

They protect these devices from theft, damage, and people accessing them without permission.

2. Digital Vaults

Digital vaults are secure systems used to store digital evidence like files, documents, logs, and forensic copies.

They use passwords and encryption (a method of protecting information) to keep the evidence safe.

3. Access Control

Access control means allowing only authorized people to access the evidence.

Passwords and other security methods help prevent people from accessing evidence without permission.

4. Write Once Read Many (WORM) Storage

WORM storage allows information to be saved once and read many times.

It prevents the stored evidence from being changed or deleted during the protected storage period.

5. Audit Logging

Audit logging means keeping a record of who accessed the evidence, when they accessed it, and what they did.

These records help investigators know who handled the evidence and support the Chain of Custody.

Conclusion

Safe boxes and digital vaults help investigators store and protect digital evidence properly.

They keep the evidence safe and reliable for use in court.
