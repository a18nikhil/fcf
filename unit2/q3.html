Q3. Explain the Reconstruction of Past Events in Computer Forensics.

Introduction

Reconstruction of past events means finding out what happened during a cybercrime by checking digital evidence.

It helps investigators understand what happened, when it happened, and how the crime took place.

1. Sources Used to Reconstruct Past Events

  • System Logs: Records of computer activities and logins.
  • File Metadata: Details about when files were created, accessed, or changed, if recorded.
  • Browsing History: Records of websites visited.
  • Emails and Messages: Records of communication between people.
  • Network Logs: Records of network connections and IP addresses.
  • Deleted Data: Deleted files and records that may still be recovered.

2. Steps in Reconstruction of Past Events

1. Collect Digital Evidence

Collect files, system logs, browsing history, and other records related to the crime.

2. Examine the Evidence

Check the collected evidence to find important activities related to the crime.

3. Check Dates and Times

Check the dates and times of activities and compare them with other records to confirm when events happened.

4. Create an Event Timeline

Arrange all important activities in time order to understand what happened first and what happened next.

5. Connect Related Events

Compare different records to find connections between activities and understand how the crime happened.

6. Verify and Record Findings

Check the findings using different evidence sources and prepare a report explaining what happened.

3. Example

Suppose confidential files are stolen from a company.

An investigator checks computer records and creates the following timeline:

  • 10:00 AM – Employee logs into the computer.
  • 10:10 AM – Confidential files are opened.
  • 10:15 AM – USB drive is connected.
  • 10:20 AM – Records show files being copied to the USB drive.

This timeline helps the investigator understand the order of activities and how the files may have been stolen.

Conclusion

Reconstruction of past events helps investigators understand the order of activities during a cybercrime. It helps explain how the crime happened using digital evidence.
