Q7. Explain the Impact of Identity Theft and Fraud as a Part of Contemporary Computer Crimes.

Introduction

Identity theft means stealing someone's personal information, such as passwords, bank details, or Aadhaar details, and using it without permission.

Online fraud means cheating people through the internet to steal money or personal information.

Both are common cybercrimes that can cause serious problems for people and businesses.

1. Impact of Identity Theft and Fraud

1. Loss of Money

Criminals may steal money from bank accounts or make payments without permission.

This can cause money problems for victims.

2. Misuse of Personal Information

Criminals may use stolen personal details to create fake accounts or commit other crimes.

3. Loss of Privacy

Private information, photos, and messages may be stolen or shared without permission.

This affects the person's privacy.

4. Mental Stress

Victims may feel worried, scared, or stressed after losing money or personal information.

5. Damage to Reputation

Criminals may use someone's identity to send fake messages or do illegal activities.

This can damage the person's reputation.

6. Loss of Trust in Online Services

People may become afraid to use online banking, shopping websites, and payment apps after experiencing fraud.

7. Problems for Businesses

Businesses may lose money and customer information because of identity theft and fraud.

This can damage their reputation and reduce customer trust.

8. Legal and Recovery Problems

Victims may need to report the crime to banks and police.

Getting stolen money back or correcting false records can take time.

2. Example

Suppose a criminal steals a person's bank login details through a fake website.

The criminal uses these details to transfer money without permission.

The victim loses money, feels stressed, and must report the fraud to the bank and police.
